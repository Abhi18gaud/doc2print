How to Connect Your Shop TV / Monitor (2 Methods)

Method 1: HDMI Cable to TV (Recommended)
  1. Connect an HDMI cable from your PC to the TV / Monitor.
  2. Press Win + P on your keyboard and select Extend.
  3. Click Launch Fullscreen TV Board button above.
  4. Drag the TV window to your TV screen and press F11 for fullscreen.
Method 2: Smart TV / FireStick / Android TV
  1. Turn on your Smart TV and open its built-in Web Browser.
  2. Type your shop's TV URL: doc2print.vercel.app/tv/...
  3. Bookmark the page and press fullscreen on TV remote.
  4. It updates automatically via Wi-Fi with instant sound alerts when prints finish!
